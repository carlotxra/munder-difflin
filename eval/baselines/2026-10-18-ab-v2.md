## What it says

- **The spawn-off notice fixes G4 on both engines**, with or without V2 (fail → pass vs the T-050
  baseline). It is on by default.
- **Copilot V1, G5:** it decided the payment-provider question itself ("I chose PayPal so
  implementation can proceed"), a fee choice that needed the human, and wrote no card ask.
  With V2 it asked on the card in the right shape. This is the gap the hard rules and the worked
  example target.
- **Claude** passed everything on both prompts. V2 cost about the same per run: median $0.32
  vs $0.30. G9 was cheaper on V2 (10 calls vs 16).
- **Not tested here:** G2, G3, G6, G11, G12 (they passed on V1 in the T-050 baseline), more than
  one run per fixture, and other engines.

## Before switching `godPromptV2` on by default

The plan's bar is "a win on at least two engines". This run shows a win on Copilot and no loss on
Claude, from one run each. A `--runs 3` repeat of G5 (and ideally all G fixtures) on both engines
would confirm it is not noise: about $6 of Claude and 18 Copilot runs per arm pair.
